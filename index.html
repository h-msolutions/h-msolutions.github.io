What Material We Can & Cannot Accept

Clear guidelines helping keep local operations safe and environmentally responsible.

✓ We Happily Take:

  • • Furniture & Mattresses (Sofas, Dressers, Beds)
  • • Major Residential Appliances (Washers, Dryers, Fridges)
  • • Yard Waste, Debris, and Brush Removal
  • • Electronics, Old Computers, & Televisions
  • • Play Structures, Trampolines, & Plastic Toys
  • • Remodeling Framing Lumber, Drywall, & General Scrap
  • • Garage, Shed, & Complete Estate Leftovers
  • • Office Equipment, Desks, Retail Counter Fixtures

✕ We Absolutely Cannot Take:

  • • Toxic, Industrial, or Hazardous Chemicals
  • • Wet Household Paints & Industrial Solvents
  • • Asbestos Insulating Materials
  • • Volatile Oils, Fuels, or Corrosives
  • • Specialized Medical Waste & Biohazards
  • • Fully Pressurized Propane Gas Tanks
