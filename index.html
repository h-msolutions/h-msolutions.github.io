Our Pledge to the Community

"We believe that service to the community goes beyond just clearing space. Our mission is to handle every item with a commitment to environmental responsibility and social impact."

  • ● Recycle First: We prioritize diverting materials from the landfill by utilizing specialized recycling channels for all recoverable goods.
  • ● Donate Always: Items that still hold value are donated to local organizations in Iredell County to support families in need.
  • ● Responsible Disposal: When items cannot be repurposed, we ensure they are handled strictly according to local environmental standards.
